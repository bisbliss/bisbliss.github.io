A little loot inspiration

Goals / Alternatives

Your BiS chase, your way.

Chasing your dream BiS set? Pick the character and spec you want to gear, build your wishlist, and put your next big drops first. Your main, off-spec, and alts can each have their own plan.

Opt into discovery and adventure with other BisBliss players who choose to share. Gear picks for your class and spec can turn up in Alternatives as you run together. Check the item and where it drops, add it if it suits your build, or pass and keep chasing your favourites.

Out on a run

Another rogue in your dungeon group has a different pair of gloves on their list. A quick look could give you a new loot target for your next run.

More progress, together

Groups

A good run for the whole party.

Got a guild full of wishlists? Open Groups, choose guildmates who want to share their goals, then pick a dungeon, raid, or boss to see who still has loot to chase there.

Build a lineup around your tank, healer, and DPS spots. BisBliss suggests guildies with shared reasons to run, favouring less competition over the same drops and useful progress across the group. Tweak the lineup, whisper your guildies, and make a plan together.

One run, more happy guildies

You need a weapon. Your healer wants a ring. Your tank is chasing a shield. One dungeon, different loot goals, more reasons to go together.

From wishlist to adventure

DungeonJournal

Find the loot. Plan the run.

Running DungeonJournal, the boss, loot, and quest guide for WoW Forever? With it installed and enabled, BisBliss automatically uses its source info to help you see where your wishlist loot comes from.

Click an item’s source in BisBliss to jump straight to its journal listing, whether that’s a boss, dungeon, or quest. Want an even quicker peek? Turn on the auto-open option to bring up the journal when you add an item with a known source.

Less searching, more adventuring

Spot a pair of gloves you like, follow the source into DungeonJournal, and see which boss to put on your hit list. Your next loot chase is only a click away.
